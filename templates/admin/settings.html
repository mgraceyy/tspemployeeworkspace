Government auto-deductions

When enabled, draft payroll runs automatically compute employee-share SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax from monthly basic salary. Manual amounts are never overwritten.

Labor law premiums

When enabled, draft payroll runs add premium pay on top of base and ordinary OT: rest-day (+30% regular / +37% OT), holiday (+100% regular / +128% OT), and night differential (+10% for 22:00–06:00 work in company timezone).
